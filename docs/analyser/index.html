MTB Dynamics Jump analyser

Rides

Pull the ride folders from the Karoo:
adb pull /sdcard/Android/data/io.github.angkyria.karoomtb/files/rides
then open the rides folder here.
Your files stay on this computer. Only the map tiles are downloaded.

    Your jump pins

    Click the map where you jumped to add a pin. Drag to move it; click it for Not a jump or delete. A pass is the track within 15 m of a pin; it is counted when a Karoo jump took off within 2.5 s.
    Export saves jump-spots.json for mtb_analyze.py --pins jump-spots.json --history rides/.
